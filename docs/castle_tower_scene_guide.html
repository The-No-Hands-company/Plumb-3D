Clear your scene with File → New Scene. Click Add Primitive → Cylinder in the top ribbon header.

Radius: 2.0 m Height: 6.0 m Segments: 16 (or 24)
Object Mode

Press Tab to switch to Edit Mode. Press 3 to enter Face Select Mode. Click the top circular circular cap face of the cylinder.

Face Mode (3)

Press I for Inset. Type 0.3 in the operator panel (or drag inward to 0.3m thickness). Press Enter or click to confirm.

Notice this creates the outer stone wall collar at the outer boundary ($r = 2.0\text{m} \to 1.7\text{m}$).

I (Inset)

With the newly inset central face automatically selected, press I again. Type 0.8 in the operator panel to define the walkway width (0.8m) and central tower roof. Confirm.

✓ You now have an annular walkway ring sandwiched between the outer wall and inner roof!

I (Inset)

Hold Alt and click on an edge of the walkway ring (or Shift-click the circular ring faces) to select all 16 ring quads. Press E for Extrude, move the cursor down and enter -0.8. Confirm.

☆ The walkway sinks safely into the tower shaft, protected by the parapet breastwork!

E (Extrude)

Select alternating faces around the top outer rim wall (skip 1 quad between each selection for 8 symmetrical teeth). Press E and enter 0.5 to extrude the crenel blocks upward around the perimeter.

E → 0.5m

Plinth Flare: Select the bottom vertices of the cylinder, press S, and scale outwards by 1.15x to give the base massive defensive weight.
Corbels: Select the ring of faces right under the top rim, press Alt+E (Extrude Normals) → 0.2m to create overhanging brackets.

S / Alt+E

In Inspector, go to the 🎨 Shading tab. Set Base Color to warm granite slate #5C5954, Roughness to 0.85, and Metallic to 0.0. In the top Viewport Shading menu, enable Cavity Shading to accentuate masonry crevices.

Blueprint: Expanding the Tower into a Complete Fortified Citadel

Once your first tower is complete, duplicate and connect elements to construct an entire fortified castle compound:

🏰 1. Curtain Walls & Ramparts

Duplicate your completed tower across the 4 corners of your citadel perimeter with Shift+D (Duplicate Object). Connect them using stretched Cube primitives with identical top parapet insets to create continuous archer walkways along the entire castle walls.

🗝 2. Gatehouse & Arch Tunnel

Place two towers closely together. Bridge the gap with a central block and carve a round archway using a Cylinder with Boolean Difference (or Knife Project). Extrude a thin grid iron portcullis inside.

🎯 3. Arrow Slits (Cross Loops)

Use Loop Cut (Ctrl+R) on the cylinder shaft. Inset a pair of narrow vertical faces, press E, and extrude inward into the tower interior to form authentic archer loops.

☀️ 4. Sunlight & Path Tracing

Add a low-angle golden Directional Light (Sun) to cast long merlon shadows across the courtyard. Press F12 to launch the Production Path Tracer with soft contact shadows and atmospheric sky dome bounce!
